# Track B: finite parameter extensions

Completed: [B01 termination sweep](B01_termination_sweep/RESULTS.md). Each future experiment must identify a frozen Track A parent and its model layer (L0 or L1), its single changed parameter, fixed quantities, provenance, acceptance conditions and limitations, using [EXPERIMENT_TEMPLATE.md](../EXPERIMENT_TEMPLATE.md). Length/loss/mismatch/termination sweeps cannot overwrite the paper-reproduction baseline or be labelled paper measurements.

Planned order: (1) termination resistance on the L1 provider, because it is the paper's own design variable; (2) length; (3) RF-loss multiplier; (4) microwave–optical index mismatch. Bandwidth is the first downward −3 dB crossing relative to f0, censored when absent (B10–B12).

Borrowed from the AES project's corner analysis: once single-variable scans exist, a small "input-uncertainty corner" study may evaluate the L1 model at the low/high edges of the A03 readout uncertainty for Z0 and loss. Corners are sensitivity results, not design guarantees, and must not be presented as process corners.
